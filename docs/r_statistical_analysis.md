# Phase 9: R Statistical Analysis

## Overview
Phase 9 introduces rigorous statistical hypothesis testing, correlation analysis, and data visualization using the R programming language. This phase validates the descriptive aggregations generated by SQL and PySpark in previous phases and establishes the mathematical significance of the observed churn indicators.

## 1. Environment & Setup
- **Language**: R (version 4.6.1)
- **Core Libraries**: `dplyr`, `ggplot2`, `readr`, `broom`, `tidyr`
- **Script Location**: `analytics/r/churn_statistical_analysis.R`
- **Outputs**: `data/processed/r/`

## 2. Dataset Processing
- **Source**: `data/raw/WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv`
- **Rows**: 7,043
- **Data Quality**: Identified and explicitly dropped 11 rows with null `TotalCharges` to calculate continuous metrics accurately, leaving 7,032 clean rows.
- **Derived Variables**: `ChurnNumeric` (1/0) and `TenureGroup` created dynamically to map against the Phase 4 SQL baseline.

## 3. Descriptive Statistics
We analyzed key continuous variables:

| Metric | Mean (Overall) | Mean (Churned) | Mean (Retained) |
|--------|----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Tenure** | 32.4 months | 18.0 months | 37.7 months |
| **MonthlyCharges** | $64.80 | $74.40 | $61.30 |
| **TotalCharges** | $2,283 (approx) | $1,532 | $2,555 |

## 4. Hypothesis Testing
To validate whether the observed differences were statistically significant (α = 0.05), we performed the following tests:

### Chi-Square Tests (Categorical Independence)
| Variable | Test Statistic | p-value | Significant? | Business Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|---------|--------------|-------------------------|
| **Contract** | 1180.0 | 7.33e-257 | Yes | Contract type is heavily associated with churn. |
| **InternetService** | 729.0 | 5.83e-159 | Yes | Internet service type significantly affects churn probability. |
| **TechSupport** | 825.0 | 7.41e-180 | Yes | Presence of tech support is strongly linked to retention. |

### Welch Two Sample t-tests (Continuous Variables)
| Variable | Test Statistic | p-value | Significant? | Business Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|---------|--------------|-------------------------|
| **Tenure** | 35.0 | 2.35e-234 | Yes | Churned customers have significantly lower tenure. |
| **MonthlyCharges** | -18.3 | 2.66e-72 | Yes | Churned customers pay significantly higher monthly rates. |
| **TotalCharges** | 18.8 | 1.15e-75 | Yes | Churned customers yield lower lifetime value. |

> **Disclaimer (Association vs. Causation):** The statistical significance proves a strong *association* between these variables and churn. It does *not* prove strict causation. For example, higher monthly charges might cause churn, or customers who select premium packages might inherently be more demanding and likely to churn.

## 5. Correlation Analysis
A Pearson correlation matrix was generated for the continuous variables:

| | Tenure | MonthlyCharges | TotalCharges |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tenure** | 1.000 | 0.247 | 0.826 |
| **MonthlyCharges** | 0.247 | 1.000 | 0.651 |
| **TotalCharges** | 0.826 | 0.651 | 1.000 |

*Insight*: TotalCharges is highly correlated with Tenure (0.826), which is mathematically expected. MonthlyCharges has a weak positive correlation with Tenure (0.247), indicating that longer-tenured customers tend to pay slightly more.
